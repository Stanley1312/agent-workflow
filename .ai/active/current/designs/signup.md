# Screen Analysis: Sign Up

## Source
`requirements/mockups/p1_signup_screen.png`

## Layout Map
1. Minimal top navigation
   - Brand left
   - Already have an account? prompt and Sign in button right
2. Left marketing panel
   - Purple patterned panel with brand mark
   - Large headline: Find what you need. Match with what they have.
   - Supporting copy about offers/wants matching
   - Testimonial with star rating
3. Right auth area
   - Centered white auth card
   - Segmented tabs: Sign up active and Sign in
   - Title and subtitle
   - Display name field
   - Email field
   - Password and confirm password fields with visibility toggles
   - Terms and privacy checkbox
   - Primary Create account button
   - Privacy footnote

## Visual Notes
- Mirrors sign-in layout but with registration-focused copy.
- Lavender/purple button and active tab underline.
- Form labels are small uppercase; helper text appears under password.

## Interaction Notes
- Missing fields, duplicate email, short password, mismatch, and unchecked terms show validation errors.
- Sign in links navigate to login.
- Successful registration creates authenticated session.
